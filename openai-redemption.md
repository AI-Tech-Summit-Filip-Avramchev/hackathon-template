# Redeem your OpenAI Pro Lite code

Each participant will receive their own **ChatGPT Pro Lite code individually over chat** from the organisers. The code gives **100% off for one month**, starting when you redeem it. Each code works once; keep yours private.

**Redeem before 26 September 2026 at 02:00 Skopje time.** This is the redemption cutoff, not the end of your free month.

> [!WARNING]
> 🟠 **Free for one month — credit card required in our tested checkout; renewal may cost money.**
>
> The organiser tested this redemption flow and was asked for **credit-card details and a billing address**, even though the amount due was **$0**. Expect to provide these details to claim the offer. We have not verified a way to redeem it without a card.
>
> **The discount ends after one month.** Later subscription charges may apply under the terms shown at checkout. After the hackathon, **cancel before your account's renewal date** unless you deliberately want to pay for continued access. Set a reminder when you redeem; do not wait until the renewal day.

## 1. Open your personal redemption link

1. Sign in to your personal account at [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) in a browser. Check the email address: use the account where you want Pro Lite.
2. Open the unique redemption link sent to you privately by the organisers.
3. If you received a raw code instead of a link, replace `CODE` in this address with your assigned code and open it:

   ```text
   https://chatgpt.com/codex/p/CODE
   ```

Do not post your code or personal redemption link in your repository, screenshots, or a public chat.

## 2. Check the offer before confirming

Before accepting, check that:

- The offer is **Pro Lite, 100% off for one month**.
- The amount due today is **$0** (or zero in the displayed currency).
- You understand the renewal date, the price after the discount, and the cancellation terms shown at checkout.

Enter the requested credit-card details and billing address directly into the checkout. Do not send these details to the organisers or anyone over chat.

**If the price is not zero, the offer is different, or the code is rejected, stop before confirming and contact your mentor privately.** If you do not have a card or do not want to provide one, ask your mentor before proceeding; no card-free workaround is confirmed.

Once everything matches, confirm redemption. Check your account's subscription details to verify activation. Use the same account in ChatGPT and Codex.

## 3. Set your cancellation reminder now

Record the **actual renewal date shown in your account** and set a reminder several days before it. Your month starts at redemption, so students who redeem on different days may have different renewal dates.

Plan to cancel after the hackathon, before renewal, unless you want to keep a paid subscription. Read any cancellation notice carefully: the available guidance does not establish whether cancelling this particular promotion ends access immediately or preserves the remaining free period.

## 4. Cancel after the hackathon

1. Sign in to the same ChatGPT account in a browser.
2. Open your account settings and find the subscription or billing section.
3. Open the subscription management screen and choose the cancellation option. Exact menu labels may vary; follow the options shown in your account.
4. Complete all confirmation steps. Check that the subscription shows cancellation or that renewal is disabled, and save the confirmation and access end date.
5. If you cannot find or complete cancellation, contact OpenAI support through the account's help options well before renewal. Let your mentor know if you need help finding them.

Closing ChatGPT, uninstalling an app, or simply stopping use does **not** cancel a subscription.

## Quick checklist

- [ ] I redeemed my assigned code on the correct account.
- [ ] Checkout showed Pro Lite at 100% off for one month and $0 due today.
- [ ] I recorded my renewal date and the price that applies afterward.
- [ ] I set a cancellation reminder.
- [ ] After the hackathon: I completed cancellation and saved the confirmation, unless I chose to continue paying.

## If you need help

Contact your mentor privately with the error message. Do not share your code or payment details in public channels. If you already have a subscription and the offer is ineligible, ask your mentor before changing your existing plan.
